### [1] [Exploring Climate-Related Anxiety Through Social Media Content](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12129)
*Karim El-Sharkawy,Zainab Rehman,Vikrant Kumar,Ardavan Shahrabi,Bobbie Williams*

Main category: cs.SI

TL;DR: 本研究利用BERTopic主题建模与RoBERTa-base GoEmotions情感分类模型，对Reddit上气候相关焦虑的大规模文本进行NLP分析，揭示讨论的核心主题结构与情感模式，并指出模型改进与青年视角拓展等未来方向。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索社交媒体讨论中表达的气候相关焦虑，理解在线气候话语不仅受讨论主题影响，也受用户互动方式塑造，为大规模社交媒体分析气候焦虑提供初步框架。

Method: 对文本数据进行预处理；采用BERTopic进行主题建模，识别反复出现的主题及其随时间演化；采用基于Transformer的RoBERTa-base GoEmotions模型进行28类情感分类。

Result: 气候相关话语围绕少量核心主题构建，主要将倡导、政策等行动导向讨论与信息性、反思性内容区分开；情感分析显示帖子中恐惧、悲伤等负面情绪更为突出，而评论往往呈现更广泛的反应，包括关怀、鼓励和中性反应。

Conclusion: 在线气候话语既受讨论主题塑造，也受用户互动与回应方式影响；本工作为通过大规模社交媒体分析理解气候相关焦虑提供了初步框架，并指出改进未来模型、扩大平台覆盖、纳入青年中心视角等机会。

Abstract: This study explores climate-related anxiety as expressed through social media discussions on Reddit. Using natural language processing techniques, we analyse large-scale textual data to identify recurring themes, emotional patterns, and how these evolve over time. Text data was preprocessed and analysed using BERTopic for topic modelling and a transformer-based model for emotion classification across 28 categories using the RoBERTa-base GoEmotions model. Results show that climate-related discourse is structured around a small number of core themes, primarily separating action-oriented discussions like advocacy and policy from informational and reflective content. Emotional analysis reveals that negative emotions such as fear and sadness are more prominent in posts, while comments often introduce a wider range of responses, including care, encouragement, and neutral reactions. These findings suggest that online climate discourse is shaped not only by the topics being discussed, but also by how users engage with and respond to one another. This work provides an initial framework for understanding climate-related anxiety through large-scale social media analysis and highlights opportunities for improving future models, expanding platform coverage, and incorporating youth-centred perspectives.

### [2] ["People can change, and patterns can be broken": Contextualizing Tradeoffs in Automated Decision-Making Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12288)
*Rabeya Bosri,Anna Harbluk Lorimer,Afrida Hossain,Vasisht Duddu,Bailey Kacsmar*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 该研究通过777人准实验，考察高后果领域中ADM系统在隐私、公平与鲁棒性之间的取舍如何被公众感知。结果显示：多数情境下人们更偏好人类决策，且偏好高度依赖情境；公众对"公平"的理解超出正式定义，常将隐私风险与易被操纵视为公平问题，并将不公平或被操纵的结果视为准确性失败。呼吁采用情境感知、以人为本的ADM设计与治理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ADM系统在抵押贷款、量刑、医疗保险、招聘等高风险领域日益普及，设计需同时保障隐私、公平与抗对抗操纵。但三者相互制约，取舍直接影响民众的生命、安全与基本权利。因此，在制定合适方案前，亟需了解公众的感知与优先排序。

Method: 开展准实验研究（N = 777）。参与者评估四个具有受控取舍的决策情境，比较人类决策（HDM）与自动决策（ADM），并考察其对隐私、公平、鲁棒性等目标的偏好与理解。

Result: 在四个情境中的三个，参与者显著偏好人类决策，强调人类判断、情境理解及纳入不可量化因素的价值。参与者偏好高度依赖情境；其对"公平"的理解超越正式定义，通过多重视角（包括隐私风险与易被操纵性）来解读公平，并将不公平或被操纵的结果视为准确性的失败。

Conclusion: 设计并治理高风险情境下的ADM系统时，应采用情境感知与以人为本的方法。不应仅以纯技术目标评估ADM系统，而应考察其取舍是否契合特定领域的期望，以及是否符合社会价值观与对伤害和公平的认知。

Abstract: Automated decision-making (ADM) systems are increasingly deployed in domains such as mortgage lending, prison sentencing, health insurance coverage, and hiring. Designing a responsible ADM system in such high-stakes domains requires ensuring privacy protection, fairness across demographic groups, and robustness against adversarial manipulation. However, prioritizing one of these objectives comes at the cost of another, forcing a choice as to which tradeoff to accept in a deployment. These tradeoffs explicitly or implicitly impact the life, safety, and fundamental rights of the people in a society, and thus, the perceptions and priorities of this population are needed before we can produce appropriate solutions. To this end, we conducted a quasi-experimental study (N = 777) in which participants evaluated four decision-making scenarios with controlled tradeoffs. Participants significantly preferred human decision-making (HDM) over ADM in three of four scenarios, emphasizing the value of human judgment, contextual understanding, and the ability to incorporate non-quantifiable factors. Furthermore, in terms of tradeoffs, our findings not only show that participants' preferences are highly context-dependent, but also that their perception of a specific objective, fairness, extends beyond formal definitions. Participants interpret fairness through multiple lenses, including privacy risks and susceptibility to manipulation, and view unfair or manipulated outcomes as failures of accuracy. Overall, our findings highlight the importance of context-aware and human-centered approaches when designing and governing ADM systems in high-stakes situations. Rather than purely technical objectives, it is essential to evaluate ADM systems based on how their tradeoffs align with specific expectations within a given domain, as well as with societal values and perceptions of harm and fairness.
