Method: 采用时序正则化NMF技术：在所有时间周期中创建一致的角色集合和一个通用的角色转移矩阵；引入正则化惩罚项，防止角色隶属度在不同时间周期之间发生剧烈变化，以增强对噪声的鲁棒性。

Result: 在五个真实世界数据集和一个合成模拟数据集上，使用工程化特征和自动生成特征进行测试。结果表明，在合适的正则化权重参数下，该方法相比其他技术显著降低了预测误差；转移矩阵的迹分析表明该方法产生了更稳定的系统，个体更倾向于停留在原角色中，减少了任意转换。

Conclusion: 所提模型能够学习时间对齐的角色，捕捉行为随时间的变化，并高效扩展到大规模稀疏图。

Abstract: This paper introduces a dynamic role discovery technique in temporal dynamic networks, utilizing temporally regularized Non-negative Matrix Factorization (NMF). Our technique differs from existing dynamic role analysis techniques by creating a consistent set of roles across all time periods, as well as a universal transition matrix that describes the probability of transitioning between roles. We also apply a regularization penalty to ensure that role membership does not change dramatically between time periods making our model more robust against real-world noise. We test our data on five real-world and one synthetically simulated dataset using both engineered and automatically generated features. We demonstrate that the proposed regularized role detection method, for appropriate regularization weight parameter reduces prediction errors compared to other techniques. Furthermore, trace analysis of the transition matrices indicates that our method yields a more stable system, that is, individuals are more likely to stay in their roles with fewer arbitrary transitions. Our model learns time-aligned roles, captures behavioral transitions over time, and scales efficiently to large and sparse graphs.
