### [1] [MetaShare: Failure-Aware Collective Self-Improvement for Open-Ended Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21887)
*Vishal Bhutani,Prasang Gupta,Sumanth Chundru,Kevin Paul*

Main category: cs.MA

TL;DR: MetaShare是一个"失败感知"的开放式自改进智能体框架：将成功与失败的智能体修改连同带符号的性能增量一起记录，跨谱系检索多样化高效策略与高置信失败经验，配合任务无关审计者注入缺失机制类别与嵌入去重排序，并以确定性选择器进行多提案门控，从而显著减少演化迭代次数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有开放式自改进智能体（如GEA的跨谱系经验共享、DGM-Hyperagents的自我改进机制编辑）存在两个问题：失败改进尝试很少被保留为可复用的跨分支知识；搜索易坍缩到冗余策略上。作者希望让智能体不仅"记住成功"，也"记住不要重犯的失败"。

Method: 1) 记录成功与失败的智能体修改及带符号性能增量（signed performance deltas）；2) 每代前从其他谱系检索多样化高效策略与高置信失败经验，实现有效机制复用与死路规避；3) 任务无关审计者（task-blind auditor）周期性注入缺失或表征不足的机制类别；4) 基于嵌入的去排序（de-ranking）降低检索冗余；5) 多个元智能体在异构温度下并行提出修改，并由确定性选择器按适用性、作用域、可编译性与规模进行门控。

Result: 在论文评审、编程、数学评分、机器人奖励设计与金融审计风险评估五类任务上，MetaShare的中位性能达到或超过DGM-Hyperagents的最终性能，并以1.7–2.9倍的更少演化迭代达到其自身最佳智能体。

Conclusion: 将失败经验作为一等可复用知识、并结合多样性保障机制（审计者注入与嵌入去重）与严格门控的选择流程，可有效提升开放式自改进智能体的演化效率，缓解策略冗余与搜索坍缩。

Abstract: Humans often learn fastest not by repeating success, but by remembering which failures not to repeat. Open-ended self-improving agents can share experience across lineages, as in GEA, or edit their own improvement mechanisms, as in DGM-Hyperagents, yet failed improvement attempts are rarely retained as reusable cross-branch knowledge, and search can collapse onto redundant strategies. We introduce MetaShare, a failure-aware framework that records successful and unsuccessful agent modifications together with signed performance deltas. Before each generation, MetaShare retrieves diverse high-performing strategies alongside high-confidence failures from other lineages, letting agents reuse effective mechanisms while avoiding known dead ends. A task-blind auditor periodically injects absent or underrepresented mechanism categories, while embedding-based de-ranking limits redundancy during retrieval. Multiple meta-agents propose modifications in parallel at heterogeneous temperatures behind a deterministic selector that gates applicability, scope, compilability, and size. Experiments across paper review, coding, math grading, robotics reward design, and financial audit-risk assessment show that MetaShare's median matches or exceeds DGM-Hyperagents' final performance and reaches its own best-performing agent in 1.7--2.9$\times$ fewer evolution iterations.

### [2] [A Panoramic Network Overview of Shortcut Impact on Power Grids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21157)
*Yunju Choi,Daekyung Lee,Heetae Kim*

Main category: physics.soc-ph

TL;DR: 在生产者与消费者空间分簇的环形电网模型中，新增长程"捷径"链路对同步稳定性的影响取决于其相对发电与用电空间布局的位置：中心—中心捷径可将平均盆地稳定度最多提升75%，而外围捷径反而可降低最多28%（类Braess退化）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 固定拓扑上的同步稳定性已有大量研究，但新增传输链路的影响如何依赖于该链路相对于发电与负荷空间组织的位置，尚不清楚。

Method: 采用简化的环形电网模型，生产与消费节点空间聚簇；在非相邻节点间添加长程"捷径"；以二阶耦合振子动力学下的盆地稳定度量化同步稳定性；在改变网络规模、功率分配、注入异质性和生产—消费布局的四种场景下比较不同捷径位置，并辅以时域仿真。

Result: 生产与消费形成两个空间分离簇时，中心—中心捷径不降低平均盆地稳定度，且相较无捷径基线最高提升75%；外围捷径虽增加连接，某些配置下反使平均盆地稳定度降低最多28%（Braess类退化）。在交替多块网络中，无单一捷径位置可获得同等显著提升，说明中心—中心优势不能直接推广。时域仿真显示中心—中心捷径下频率偏差衰减更快。

Conclusion: 新增连接对同步稳定性的效果关键取决于新链路相对于发电与消费空间组织的位置，链路选址而非仅链路数量决定稳定性收益。

Abstract: Synchronization of coupled oscillators on complex networks underlies critical functions in natural and engineered systems, including power-grid operation. While synchronization stability on fixed network topologies has been studied extensively, less is known about how an added transmission link affects stability depending on where it is placed relative to generation and consumption. We address this question using simplified ring-network power-grid models in which producer and consumer nodes are spatially organized into clusters. Long-range transmission links, which we refer to as shortcuts, are added between non-adjacent nodes, and synchronization stability is quantified by basin stability in second-order coupled-oscillator dynamics. We compare shortcut placements across four scenarios varying network size, power allocation, power-injection heterogeneity, and producer--consumer arrangement. When producers and consumers form two spatially separated clusters, center-to-center shortcuts do not reduce the mean basin stability in the configurations examined and increase it by up to 75\% relative to the shortcut-less baseline. Peripheral shortcuts instead reduce mean basin stability by up to 28\% in some configurations despite the added connection, producing Braess-like degradation. In alternating multi-block networks, however, no single shortcut placement provides the same substantial enhancement, indicating that the center-to-center advantage does not directly generalize to more complex spatial arrangements. Representative time-domain simulations further show faster decay of frequency deviations for center-to-center than for peripheral shortcuts. These results demonstrate that the effect of added connectivity depends critically on where the new link is placed relative to the spatial organization of generation and consumption.

### [3] [A Systematic and Validated Translation of the Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale to Chinese](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21541)
*Matthias Schmidmaier,Jingyao Zheng,Jie Song,Lik-Hang Lee,Sven Mayer*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 本文将技术共情感知量表（PETS）翻译并验证为普通话（简体中文）版本，基于回译法、专家小组评审与N=400的实证检验，确认其保留原双因子结构并满足形态、度量与标量等值性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 共情行为评估在人机交互尤其是情感与心理健康支持中日益重要，且共情的表达与感知存在文化与语言差异；鉴于庞大的中文使用群体与跨文化HCI研究需求，需要一份经过验证的中文版PETS。

Method: 采用既有PETS翻译所用的回译方法：多次独立翻译、专家小组评审，并在英语与中文样本共N=400名被试上进行实证测试；使用验证性因子分析与测量不变性检验。

Result: 中文版PETS保留原始双因子结构且信度高，获得形态、度量与标量不变性支持；共情性系统行为在两种语言下评分等价，而非共情行为在中文被试中获得显著更高评分。

Conclusion: 该验证版中文PETS可供研究者与从业者评估中文用户的共情感知，支持文化适配的共情技术开发；同时提示"共情缺失"相较"共情存在"在不同文化间解读一致性更低。

Abstract: The Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale (PETS) allows evaluation of empathic behavior in intelligent systems, a concept increasingly applied in human-AI interaction, particularly in emotional and mental health support applications. To support intercultural research and development, PETS is already available in multiple languages, as research suggests cultural and language-based differences in the expression and perception of empathy. Given the substantial Chinese-speaking population and the growing focus on cross-cultural HCI research, we present a validated translation of PETS into Putonghua (Mandarin) with Simplified Chinese. Based on established back-translation methodologies as used in previous PETS translations, our process included multiple independent translations, expert panel reviews, and empirical testing with a total of N=400 participants from an English and a Chinese sample. Through confirmatory factor analysis and invariance testing we show that the Chinese PETS retains the original two-factor structure with high reliability, with support for configural, metric, and scalar invariance. Beyond validation, empathic system behavior was rated equivalently across languages, while non-empathic behavior drew substantially higher ratings from Chinese participants, suggesting its absence is read less consistently across cultures than its presence. This validated Chinese version enables researchers and practitioners to assess empathy perceptions among Chinese-speaking users, supporting culturally appropriate empathic technology development.

</details>
